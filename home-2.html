Fish Care & Maintenance

A Simple Routine for Healthy Fish.

Most problems start with skipped basics. This is the schedule our team keeps on the store tanks.

Read Care Guides

Daily

2 minutes
  • Feed small amounts once or twice — only what's eaten in 2 minutes.
  • Count fish and look for clamped fins, spots or heavy breathing.
  • Check the heater light and thermometer reading.
Shop thermometers & food

Weekly

20–30 minutes
  • Change 20–25% of the water with dechlorinated, temperature-matched water.
  • Vacuum the gravel and wipe algae from the front glass.
  • Test ammonia, nitrite, nitrate and pH.
Shop test kits & conditioner

Monthly

45 minutes
  • Rinse filter sponges in old tank water — never tap water.
  • Trim plants and remove decaying leaves.
  • Clean tubing and impellers; replace carbon if you use it.
Shop filter media
