KEEP THE SYSTEM STEADY

Fish Care & Maintenance

A healthy aquarium is mostly about consistency. Small, repeatable checks keep the environment stable and make problems easier to spot early.

DAILY

Observe & top up

Watch fish behaviour, check equipment is running and top up evaporated water when appropriate.

WEEKLY

Test & maintain

Check key parameters, inspect filter flow, remove debris and perform your planned water change.

MONTHLY

Clean & review

Inspect media, tubing, heaters and lights, then review whether your equipment still suits the livestock.
